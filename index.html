Taught by people who've done the work.

Every Aura teacher has 500+ hours of training and at least five years of teaching behind them. With classes capped at 18 mats, there's room for them to actually see your practice and adjust it by hand.

Built for practitioners who want more

You practise three or four times a week and you've stopped getting better. That isn't a motivation problem. It's 40 mats edge to edge, sequencing that feels random, and teachers who never cue the next variation. Aura is built the other way round.

Warmth, not punishment

Infrared heat holds the room at 32 to 36°C and warms your body rather than the air. It feels like a warm day, not a sauna, so you can practise longer and with more control.

Eighteen mats, expert teachers

Every class is capped at 18 mats, so teachers can see you, cue the advanced variation and give hands-on adjustments. A consent card on every mat lets you choose.

Progress you can actually see

A 20-minute assessment places you correctly from the start, and Progressions break each pose into stages, so every class builds on the last.

Progressions

Eight weeks. One pose. Broken into stages.

Each Progression is a small cohort working towards one specific pose, from a freestanding handstand to forearm stand or a drop-back. You build the strength, range and control each stage needs, so you get there without forcing it.

Questions, answered

What people usually ask before their first class at Aura.

Aura isn't a beginner studio. Our classes are built for people with a few years of practice who want more than all-levels classes give them. If you're not sure where you sit, the assessment will tell you, and if you're not quite at intermediate yet, The Bridge is a 4-week pathway that gets you there.

Traditional hot yoga heats the air, often to around 40°C with high humidity. Infrared heat warms your body directly, so the room can sit at a gentler 32 to 36°C. You get warm, responsive muscles without the punishing, sauna-like feel.

Heated classes sit between 32 and 36°C. Restore runs at a lower heat, because it's designed for recovery.

Your intro pass includes a free 20-minute practice assessment with one of our teachers. They'll see where your practice is now and recommend a class mix or a Progression to start with.

It depends on where you start. Of the 140 students who've completed a Progression, 70% landed their target pose within two cohorts. Each Progression breaks the pose into stages, so you build the strength and control to hold it rather than forcing it.

Progressions are staged so you never jump ahead of what your body is ready for. Our teachers are trained in anatomy, adjustments are always by consent, and we work with a physio partner.

The 14-day intro pass is $59 and includes your assessment. After that, unlimited membership is $72 a week on a 3-month minimum or $85 a week with no lock-in. Drop-ins are $38 and a 10-class pack is $330.
